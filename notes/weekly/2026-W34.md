### 8. Diagnostic Foundation for Evaluating LLMs' Research Integrity as Co-Scientists

### 9. Large Language Models Can Follow Instructions, But Not Many at Once: Phase Transitions in Compositional Constraint Satisfaction

- source：arXiv cs.AI
- url：https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12426

- importance：5/5
